Algorithm changes supported by this audit

  1. Discover candidates before GMM exclusion. 11528: 226/228 reads with the recurring 21-bp insertion are excluded by the default GMM; two are assigned to WT. Length clustering should propose candidates, not limit the sequence search.
  2. Require consistent complete net length. Several apparent small gaps occur on larger-ITD molecules. Confirm both outer flanks and the whole local haplotype, rather than interpreting one of multiple alignment gaps as an independent ITD.
  3. Preserve sequence and breakpoint within each length. 13462 has two recurrent 45-bp sequence forms, differing at one junction base, plus 48 bp. Length-only splitting cannot resolve this heterogeneity. Validate the supported full haplotypes and preserve uncertainty about the base difference rather than discard the shared insertion-length evidence.
  4. Rescue and display unresolved candidate clusters. 13462 already reaches a separate DADA2 cluster, but mixed 45-bp sequences yield an unresolved consensus that is skipped. Recluster the supported haplotypes and display rejected-candidate counts/ambiguity even when the remaining consensus has zero Ns.
  5. Separate discovery thresholds from reporting thresholds. A small clone beside a large peak can fail 15% of parent even with adequate total support. Use absolute read support and local error controls alongside global support; retain subthreshold candidates for review.
  6. Add explicit short-insertion review mode. For exploratory min size 3, WT tolerance must be <3 (e.g.1); search the WT peak and rescue before the no-non-WT early exit. Blindly lowering min size increases short error calls; require reproducible sequence and a local background comparison.
  7. Keep all eligible reads in final competition. Use WT + known ITDs + read-supported candidates; report ambiguous/rejected counts and all-trimmed support as well as the classified-read AF denominator.
  8. Do not treat every recurrent error haplotype as a clone. Neighboring -1/-2 bp versions of large ITDs can recur and be strand-biased. Use net size, complete sequence, quality/strand controls, independent PCR evidence, and background rather than automatic acceptance.

Methods and practical limits

Scope

Identity mapping

Input integrity

FASTQ provenance
No original per-sample FASTQs were supplied in FRAGMENT_DATA. Region and primer-trimmed FASTQs were regenerated from each corresponding BAM and retained under <sample>/fastq. They are the same underlying sequencing evidence, not an independent assay.
BAM selection
Use combined _2runs/_merged/_all where available; exclude _1/_5 dilution BAMs. Primary alignments and MAPQ >=20 were inspected for CIGAR evidence.
Trimming
Cutadapt production primer settings, min read length 320 for discovery (default 330 replayed separately), maximum 666. All read IDs and qualities retained.
Discovery
Compare a permissive gapped aligner, production aligner, original BAM CIGARs, and exact outer-anchor net-size/haplotype evidence. No GMM or ASV gate is imposed on all-read discovery.
Outer anchors
WT[25:40] and WT[300:315], 0-based half-open. Window length in WT is 290 bp. Net insertion size is the complete observed window length minus 290, including all indels between anchors. Exact-anchor reads are a subset and their counts are conservative sequence evidence, not AF.
Candidate panel
Complete exact anchored haplotypes with >=5 reads near target (+/-2.5 bp), up to three per target, plus known ITDs. Primary AF for the four candidate-bearing cases uses the clean panel. Extended diagnostic panels also contain recurrent neighboring error haplotypes; these are not accepted as biological ITDs.
Validation
Current pipeline competitive alignment and breakpoint-gap checks on every trimmed read, with sequence caching expanded back to every ID and original strand. AF denominator is validated/classified reads; support fraction using all trimmed reads is also provided.
Not corroborated
No reproducible full haplotype at the requested size in these data. This is not a calibrated negative test or proof of a fragment-analysis artifact. Tiny broad length tails and a few inconsistent reads are insufficient for confirmation.
Small insertions
Exploratory discovery includes insertions >=3 bp and evaluates the below-minimum cases. None of the supplied below-12-bp targets is corroborated by a coherent full haplotype.
Parameter experiments
Baseline replay: current default discovery with min reporting AF=0.005. Sensitive replay: min AF .001, min GMM fraction .001, max SD 10, subpeak fraction .01, min haplotype reads 10, max ITDs 10, min read length 320, min ITD 3, WT tolerance 1. These are review experiments, not a validated production configuration.
Parameter results
11528: baseline reports 159,36,33 bp; sensitive settings add the supported 21-bp call at AF 2.303%, and additional low-level 24-bp/36-bp candidates. 13462: baseline reports only 48 bp; sensitive settings still skip the unresolved 45-bp population and add a 39-bp candidate at 0.187%. This demonstrates that broad parameter relaxation alone does not solve all missed calls; the additional candidates were not accepted automatically.
Limitations
One supplied sequencing dataset per case; no UMIs or independent extraction/PCR replicates established. Distinct read IDs do not establish distinct original DNA molecules. Fragment electropherograms/raw peak traces and the original pipeline commands were unavailable.
Shared allele
11528 21-bp allele matches the dominant 21-bp allele in 12156. This can reflect a recurrent hotspot allele; shared sequence alone cannot distinguish biological recurrence from cross-sample carryover.
13462 failure trace
Baseline DADA2 infers two ASVs from the 503-read ITD peak. The 227-read ITD_1_H2 cluster has 170 extracted 45-bp insertions, including 103 of one sequence and 56 of another. Its context consensus is unresolved and skipped. The ambiguity summary then reports 0 Ns because it summarizes only surviving candidates.
13462 sequence forms
The reconstructed 381-bp alleles differ at one base, T versus G at full-allele index 280. Their exact anchored supports are 57 reads (30+/27-) and 31 (15+/16-), with median base qualities 33 and 39 at that position. This supports the insertion-length population but does not establish whether the base difference is biological or technical.
13462 read fate
Of the 211 reads assigned to the 45-bp candidate forms by the focused competitive panel, the baseline replay classifies 176 as validated support for its 48-bp reference. Another 28 prefer that reference but fail gap checks; seven prefer WT but fail checks. Candidate loss therefore also reallocates support to the surviving ITD.
Small-gap example
In 11551 the production aligner finds 163 recurring GAGA insertions of 4 bp, but most linked reads have net gains of 12-18 bp, matching the 15-bp major ITD/error tail rather than a distinct 4-bp molecule. Recurrent alignment gaps alone can be misleading.
